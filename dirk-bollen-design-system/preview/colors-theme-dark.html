Dark Theme Surfaces

Dark blocks use --db-ink (#211B43) as background. Hero sections, CTAs, footer bands, and slide dark frames all use this surface.

Dark Surface Tokens

Background Dark

Foreground Inverse

--fg-inverse → #FFFFFF — all body text on dark

Muted on Dark

rgba(255,255,255,0.55) — secondary text on dark

Border on Dark

rgba(255,255,255,0.12) — dividers on dark

Accents on Dark

Pink — CTA / Signal

--accent · #EE4280 — primary action on dark

Cyan — Focus / Present

--accent-alt · #45C4D9 — focus ring and present-state on dark
